# Slide checklist

Run this over a deck before a talk.

## Per slide
- One idea. If a slide has two, split it.
- Title states the takeaway, not the topic. "Clearance halved in poor metabolizers", not
  "Pharmacokinetics".
- No paragraph you would read aloud verbatim. Slides are for the audience's eyes while you
  talk, not a script.
- Text large enough to read from the back row. If in doubt, larger.
- High contrast. Dark on light or light on dark, not mid-gray on white.
- A figure or diagram wherever the point has shape. Label it so it reads in five seconds.
- No more than a few bullets, and none of them full sentences. Prefer a visual to a list.

## Across the deck
- The one load-bearing idea is stated on an early slide and again on the last.
- A reset every ten minutes or so: a question, demo, example, or section marker.
- Fewer slides than the time suggests. Aim to finish early.
- The open and the close are the strongest slides.
- No "Outline" slide read line by line, and no "Thank you / Questions" slide as the final
  image. End on the takeaway.

## Live demo, if any
- Rehearsed on the actual machine and network you will use.
- A fallback (screenshots or a recording) in case it breaks.
- Big font in the terminal or editor.
